Focus Islands

A personal, local-first macOS menu bar game: click the icon to open the game window, with no Dock icon.

MVP

  • Flexible work and break timers, with gentle, repeatable break reminders and snooze or skip controls.
  • Use recent input and screen lock to infer time away; allow manual break confirmation. Never record keystrokes or mouse movements.
  • A Chrome extension nudges or redirects visits to chosen distracting sites during work, with a deliberate bypass.
  • Work and actual breaks grow a pet, room, or garden; tune rewards and the art style later.

Morning loop

Optionally reflect on yesterday’s work and break lengths, enter today’s expected meetings, then see focus and break suggestions around them in a read-only calendar. The plan is guidance, not a rule. A task list is outside this MVP.

History and feedback

Keep an append-only local record of plans and revisions, timer and break events, reminders, aggregate activity signals, and reflections. Derive rewards and views from those facts so the rules can change later. Show a planned-versus-actual daily timeline and use patterns to suggest better block lengths.

References

  • Clippy — MIT; macOS pet overlay, Pomodoro timer, idle behavior, and stretch reminders. Review its input-monitoring approach before reusing it.
  • OpenDesktopPets — MIT; small, local macOS dog/cat overlay with reusable visual ideas.
  • Stretchly — BSD-2-Clause; cross-platform break scheduling and idle-aware breaks.
  • Desktop Pet Engine — Apache-2.0; native macOS pet rendering and asset pipeline. Check the separate media restrictions.
